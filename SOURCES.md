# Travel Alerts.ke — Source Registry

**Purpose:** A curated starting directory for research sources that can establish or corroborate developments affecting travellers across Africa and globally.

**Operating rule:** The latest master guide and `TRAVELALERTS_RESEARCH.md` in [Chatgpt.-Md](https://github.com/siiro-byte/Chatgpt.-Md) govern source selection, verification and publication. This registry is a source directory, not evidence that an event is happening now.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Primary authorities:** the government, regulator, airport, airline or operator responsible for the specific country, location or service.
2. **Independent corroboration:** established news organisations and specialist reporting.
3. **Traveller signals:** Reddit and social posts are discovery leads only; never proof of a policy, closure, incident or disruption.

## National authority discovery checklist

For every country relevant to a candidate alert, identify and use the current official source for the specific subject:

- Foreign affairs ministry and official travel advisories.
- Immigration, citizenship and border-control authority.
- Health ministry, public-health institute and national disease-surveillance authority.
- Civil aviation authority and air-navigation service provider.
- Airport operator and affected airline.
- Police, emergency management and disaster-response authority.
- National meteorological service.
- Road, rail, port and ferry operator or transport regulator.
- Tourism ministry or national tourism board for visitor access and destination information.
- Official gazette or legal publication for regulatory changes.

Do not assume one ministry is authoritative for every subject. Record the exact issuing authority and original notice.

## Traveller-signal sources

- Reddit: https://www.reddit.com/
- Relevant destination, airline, airport and transport-operator social accounts.
- Traveller forums and local reporting.

Capture the original post URL, post date, event date if known, exact location and whether the author reports first-hand experience. Seek independent confirmation. Multiple reposts of one claim are not independent corroboration.

## Required source record

For each source actually used in research, record:

| Field | Required entry |
|---|---|
| Source name | Official organisation, publisher or original poster |
| Direct URL | Exact page or notice, not just the homepage where possible |
| Country / region | Geographic scope |
| Category | Safety, health, immigration, aviation, transport, weather, tourism or media |
| Source class | Primary authority, independent media, specialist source or traveller lead |
| What it establishes | The precise fact supported by the source |
| Published / updated | Date shown by the source |
| Event date | When the event occurred or measure takes effect |
| Checked | Date and time checked in EAT |
| Current status | Active, ended, superseded, unclear or not applicable |
| Confidence | CONFIRMED, PARTLY CONFIRMED, CONFLICTING, UNVERIFIED or STALE |

## Publication safeguards

- Confirm that the item is current and not already covered unless there is a material update.
- Separate publication date, event date and effective date.
- Confirm who is affected, the precise location and practical traveller impact.
- Do not infer a countrywide risk from a local incident.
- Do not infer a closure, cancellation or entry ban without direct evidence.
- Keep unresolved claims as internal monitoring leads, not confirmed public alerts.
- Prefer the original official notice over an article summarising it.
- Cite the exact page supporting each published claim.
